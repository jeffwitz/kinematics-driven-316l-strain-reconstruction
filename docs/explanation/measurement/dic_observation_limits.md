# What DIC observes, and what it does not

**Mode:** explanation  
**Domain:** measurement

DIC observes image motion through an observation chain. It provides measured
components and uncertainty on a grid; it does not directly observe stress,
plastic slip or equilibrium. The observation operator, masks and axis mapping
must therefore remain explicit when a mechanical field is compared with DIC.

See the stable contracts in {doc}`../../reference/observation_operator` and
{doc}`../../reference/dic_axis_conventions`.
