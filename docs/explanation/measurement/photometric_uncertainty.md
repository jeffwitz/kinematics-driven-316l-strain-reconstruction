# Photometric uncertainty and transfer limits

**Mode:** explanation  
**Domain:** measurement

Image texture, filtering, interpolation and the DISFlow chain determine which
spatial and temporal modes can be transferred from a mechanical field to a
DIC observation. This is why a digital-twin correlation can succeed while a
mechanical ranking fails after passing through the real observation operator.

The evidence registry records those two statements separately; neither should
be collapsed into a claim of constitutive identifiability.
