# Why a local law can miss spatial interaction

**Mode:** explanation  
**Domain:** reconstruction

A point-local constitutive law sees the orientation and history at one material
point. It does not, by itself, communicate neighbouring morphology. When the
measured field spreads deformation over a band or around a defect, a local
baseline can produce the right global reaction while placing the strain in the
wrong pixels.

The historical micromorphic/nonlocal branch investigates this missing length
scale. Its status and limits are recorded in
{doc}`../evidence/negative_results` and the nonlocal references linked from
{doc}`../../reference/index`.
