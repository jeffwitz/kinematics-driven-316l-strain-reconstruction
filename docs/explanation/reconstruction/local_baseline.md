# What the local baseline can establish

**Mode:** explanation  
**Domain:** reconstruction

The J2/Ludwik local reconstruction is a useful baseline: it checks data
preparation, boundary conventions, equilibrium and field metrics before a
crystal law is introduced. Its strong localisation and narrow morphology are
scientific limitations of the model, not evidence that the DIC data are wrong.

The baseline is therefore retained as a historical and diagnostic reference.
It must not be confused with the current SRIX production path. Definitions of
the comparison metrics are in {doc}`../../reference/evidence/validation_metrics`.
