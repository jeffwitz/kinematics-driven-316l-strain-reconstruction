# Negative results are part of the evidence

**Mode:** explanation  
**Domain:** evidence

The repository records several useful boundaries:

- the local J2 baseline misses the measured morphology;
- Méric results require explicit temporal-refinement interpretation;
- CPS4R was not qualified for the plastic campaigns;
- the registered EBI-TET SRIX case was falsified;
- REGM ranking does not currently transfer through the real DIC observation
  chain.

These outcomes constrain what may be claimed. They do not delete the methods
or prevent a future, separately qualified formulation.
