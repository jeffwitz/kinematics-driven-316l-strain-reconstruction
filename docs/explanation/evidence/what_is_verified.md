# What is verified, supported and still open

**Mode:** explanation  
**Domain:** evidence

Verified claims are backed by reproducible tests with explicit tolerances and
artefacts. Supported claims have consistent evidence but a remaining
qualification boundary. Falsified or unqualified approaches remain visible as
negative results. Unknown means that the available data do not discriminate
the hypotheses; it is not permission to select a convenient convention.

The definitions and links to primary artefacts are maintained in
{doc}`../../reference/evidence/evidence_registry` and the Evidence portal.
