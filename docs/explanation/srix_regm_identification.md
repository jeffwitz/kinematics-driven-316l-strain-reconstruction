# Fast SRIX identification by a reconditioned equilibrium gap

**Category: Explanation.** Why SRIX-REGM can screen crystal-plasticity
parameters without a nonlinear mechanical solve in every objective evaluation,
and where the approximation currently fails.

## The question

Full FEMU places SRIX inside a nonlinear equilibrium solve for every candidate
parameter vector. That is the definitive comparison, but it is too expensive
to explore several crystal-plasticity parameters blindly. SRIX-REGM asks a
cheaper local question first:

> If the measured strain history were replayed through a candidate SRIX law,
> how large a first-order displacement correction would be required to restore
> weak mechanical equilibrium?

The method is a ranking surrogate. It is not claimed to be mathematically
equivalent to FEMU.

## Causal constitutive replay

The nodal history supplies engineering strains through the same two-subcell
kinematic operator as the forward solver. For each parameter vector
`theta = (tau0, R, Q, b)`, the qualified plane-stress SRIX batch is replayed
from state zero. Internal variables are committed between every accepted
increment; scoring a subset of states never resets the material history.

The resulting sample stresses are assembled with the existing weak operator:

```text
f(theta) = B^T W sigma(theta).
```

Only interior degrees of freedom enter `f`: boundary terms are unknown
Dirichlet reactions, not equilibrium errors. No strong numerical divergence of
the noisy stress field is formed.

## Elastic reconditioning

A constant, once-factorised elastic reference operator converts the force-like
gap into a displacement-like correction:

```text
delta_u(theta) = -K0^-1 f(theta).
```

Here `K0` uses the EBSD-oriented cubic plane-stress elasticity at each point.
The sign is fixed by a mechanical test: adding `delta_u` must reduce the weak
residual of a perturbed elastic field. The observable residual is

```text
r(theta) = W_D O(delta_u(theta)),
```

where `O` is the qualified displacement observation transfer and `W_D` its
noise whitener. `K0`, measured strains, EBSD orientation arrays, transfer and
whitener are prepared once. A parameter evaluation contains material replay
and cheap substitutions, but zero global Newton and zero global Krylov solve.

## What the exact twin establishes

An M8 digital twin generated by the complete nonlinear SRIX solver provides a
known truth. With exact displacement and identity observation, the true REGM
RMS is `1.474e-13 mm`; a frozen perturbed initial point gives `3.143e-8 mm`.
Least squares returns to `1.412e-13 mm`, with `0.248 %` log-parameter error in
the retained singular subspace.

The sensitivity SVD has four non-zero directions, but spans more than four
orders of magnitude. The weakest direction is mainly an opposite-sign `Q/b`
combination. It is formally visible in exact arithmetic and expected to be the
first direction lost under measurement noise.

One exact REGM evaluation costs `2.90 s`, against `124.48 s` for the generating
forward trajectory. Scaling tests show that constitutive replay, not
`K0^-1`, dominates the cost; a new FFT inverse would not address the present
bottleneck.

## What the observation chain changes

The same twin becomes a negative identification test if the measured DIC
transfer is injected into the constitutive replay. The true parameters no
longer minimize the REGM objective:

| observation | truth RMS | lower admissible RMS reached |
|---|---:|---:|
| transfer only | `2.132e-7 mm` | `1.300e-7 mm` |
| transfer, measured noise and whitening | `1.741e-3` | `1.346e-3` |

This does not mean that SRIX integration has failed. The exact test uses the
same constitutive backend and recovers the truth. The issue is the placement of
the observation operator: `B O(u*)` is replayed by a nonlinear, history-dependent
threshold law, so a small displacement transfer can change active systems and
all subsequent internal variables.

The preregistered A/B/C/D ablation makes this distinction quantitative. With
the raw mechanical twin as replay input (B), scoring the correction through
either periodic or affine-preserving observation retains the observed FEMU
ranking (`rho=0.950` and `0.940`). With the transferred history as replay input
(C), the ranking collapses to `rho=0.338` even when the correction is scored by
the identity. The current combined path (D-affine) gives `rho=0.326`.

At the truth, the transferred-input path creates a pseudo-displacement RMS of
`4.067e-7 mm`, `11.64` times the candidate spread. The periodic-versus-
affine-preserving scoring difference is therefore secondary for this failure.

## A twin-only latent-history upper bound

The next diagnostic did not alter REGM or SRIX. On the exact twin, the missing
history `u* - O(u*)` was decomposed by snapshot POD and progressively added back
before the constitutive replay. The score always used `O(delta_u)`, so this
experiment tests only how much latent kinematics is needed by the replay.

The observed-input ranking starts at `rho=0.326`. It reaches `0.577` at rank
three, `0.708` at rank four and `0.859` at rank five; the first five modes carry
`99.9897 %` of the missing-history energy. The full rank is `162` and gives
`rho=0.940`. The mode-by-mode curve is not monotone, so energy alone is not a
sufficient selection rule.

```{figure} ../_static/evidence/srix_regm_latent_modes.png
:alt: Spearman ranking, truth residual and recovered missing-history energy versus latent POD rank.
:width: 95%

Twin-only latent-mode upper bound. No new FEMU solve was launched.
```

This is strong evidence for a low-dimensional latent-history mechanism, not a
validated reconstruction of real DIC. The basis used `u*`, which is unavailable
experimentally. The next legitimate gate is to construct candidate weak modes
from `O`, `K0` and the qualified observation/noise model alone, then repeat the
same twin ranking and transfer tests without using `u*` to define the basis.

## Relation to complete FEMU

On twenty preregistered off-truth exact-twin candidates, REGM and full FEMU
rankings agree well: Spearman is `0.866`, log-objective Pearson is `0.878`, and
three candidates are common to both best-five sets. The median speedup is
`4.94x`; difficult forward candidates gain much more because REGM avoids their
adaptive global Newton path.

```{figure} ../_static/evidence/srix_regm_femu_ranking.png
:alt: Exact-space REGM objective against full-FEMU displacement error and the corresponding ranks.
:width: 95%

Exact-space ranking on the frozen twenty-candidate population.
```

This is evidence that the weak reconditioned gap is a useful local surrogate
in exact kinematic space. It does not erase the observation bias.

The same population was therefore replayed after the qualified observation
chain. The transfer-only ranking fails: Spearman is `0.326`, log-Pearson is
`0.276`, and only two candidates are common to the best-five sets. The noisy,
whitened ranking formally passes, but its FEMU objective has coefficient of
variation only `9.1e-5`: the common measured-noise realization dominates the
parameter signal. The frozen rule required both levels to pass.

The current decision is consequently **NO-GO before P43**. More optimizer
iterations cannot correct an objective whose candidate ordering disagrees with
the definitive forward solve.

## Parameters and identifiability

Positive parameters are expressed in logarithmic coordinates. Central finite
differences build the residual Jacobian, not merely a scalar cost gradient.
Its SVD determines which combinations are visible. `C` and `d` remain fixed
for the monotonic P43 history unless an explicit sensitivity study adds
independent directions. A near-null direction is reported as non-identifiable;
it is never assigned a unique value by a prior and then described as measured.

## Claim boundary

**Verified:** weak assembly, correction sign, Kelvin/engineering/Voigt energy
consistency, deterministic SRIX transactions, exact-twin sensitivity and
recovery.

**Supported:** exact-space REGM ranks nearby SRIX laws similarly to complete
FEMU and is much cheaper on difficult nonlinear trajectories.

**Refuted for the present formulation:** unbiased recovery and faithful FEMU
ranking after the qualified DIC observation transfer.

**Not demonstrated:** identifiable P43 parameters or equivalence between REGM
and FEMU.

The machine-readable evidence and frozen decisions are listed in
`validation/srix_regm_worklog.md`. A final full forward solve with the
identified parameters remains mandatory even after every surrogate gate
passes.
