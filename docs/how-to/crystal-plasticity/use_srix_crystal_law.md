# Use the SRIX crystal law

**Mode:** how-to  
**Domain:** crystal-plasticity

Use the central SRIX parameter set, twelve FCC systems and the point-wise EBSD
orientation source. Confirm that the 3-D law is closed by all three transverse
traction equations before reading slip maps. The stable options and
transaction contract are in
{doc}`../../reference/numerics/native_srix_backend`; the constitutive equations
are explained in {doc}`../../explanation/constitutive/forest_rubin_srix`.
