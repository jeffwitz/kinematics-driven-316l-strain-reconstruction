# Milestone 2 — results: the local-coefficient inverse is identifiable

Against `validation/local_coefficient_inverse_preregistration.md`, thresholds
frozen before the runs. Nothing here was rerun after seeing a number, and the
two harness defects found along the way are recorded rather than quietly fixed.

## Verdict

| gate | registered | measured | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — the transpose is the transpose | `<= 1e-10` | **5.5e-16** | pass |
| 2 — the gradient, `q = 1` | `<= 1e-5`, V-shaped | **4.9e-9**, V-shaped | pass |
| 2 — the gradient, `q = 4` | `<= 1e-5`, V-shaped | **7.6e-9**, V-shaped | pass |
| 3 — twin recovery, `q = 1` | `>= 3` decades, field `<= 5 %` | **10.0 decades, 0.021 %** | pass |

| 6 — the real DIC objective | after 1-5 | **not run** | — |

**The local plastic representation is identifiable.** At `q = 1`, 64
coefficients on a 64-square grid, a twin generated by the exact forward model is
recovered to `2.1e-4` relative field error through 550 solves in 15 minutes,
with the median warm solve costing **one** Newton iteration.

## The two things worth knowing beyond the pass

### The inverse is well conditioned at `q = 1`, and that is a measured fact

The singular spectrum of the parameter-to-observable map `du/da`, 36
coefficients on 48 square:

```text
condition number                     2.0e2
directions above 1e-1 of the leading   21
                 1e-2                  35
                 1e-3                  36  (all of them)
```

No nullspace, nothing hiding below the noise of the computation. This matters
because a descent that stalls and a problem that is unidentifiable look
identical on a convergence plot, and only one of them is a property of the
physics. Exact Gauss-Newton — the least-squares step through the SVD, no line
search, no curvature estimate — confirms it independently:

| step | objective | field error |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3.47e-10 -> 4.63e-14 | 7.6 % |
| 2 | 4.63e-14 -> 1.39e-16 | 0.10 % |
| 3 | 1.39e-16 -> 1.57e-24 | 0.00 % |
| 4 | 1.57e-24 -> 5.34e-29 | 0.00 % |

Three steps to machine precision. The data determine the coefficients.

### Enriching the local modes breaks the basis, by construction

At `q = 4` — degree one in each axis, 144 coefficients on the same grid — the
same spectrum reads:

```text
condition number                     4.1e15
directions above 1e-6 of the leading   121  of 144
```

Twenty-three directions are numerically absent. **The degeneracy is in the
parameterisation, not in the mechanics**, and the two counts agree exactly: the
assembly map `a -> Delta p` alone, with no mechanics whatsoever, has the same 23
null directions.

| degree | coefficients | basis condition | exactly null |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 36 | **3.4** | 0 |
| 1 | 144 | 5.9e17 | 23 |
| 2 | 324 | 2.9e18 | 68 |

The mechanism is the partition of unity's own virtue. Bilinear hats reproduce
linear functions exactly, so

```text
sum_j w_j(x) = 1        and        sum_j w_j(x) (x - x_j) = 0
```

— the second verified at `4.2e-17`, machine zero. Enriching a partition of unity
with the polynomials it already reproduces produces a linearly dependent basis.
This is the known linear-dependency pathology of partition-of-unity and
generalised finite element enrichment, arriving here exactly on schedule.

It is a defect of the construction chosen for this milestone and not of the
programme: the remedy is standard — orthogonalise the enriched modes against the
constants, use a flat-top partition of unity, or truncate the assembly map's own
SVD. **None of that was done here**, because it was not in the registered scope
and because the `q = 1` result already answers the question the milestone was
built to ask.

## What was left out, and why

**The `q = 4` twin was not run.** With 23 null directions in the basis, a
recovery experiment measures the pseudo-inverse's treatment of a rank-deficient
parameterisation rather than anything about the physics. It becomes informative
once the basis is orthogonalised, and not before.

**Gate 6, the real DIC objective, was not reached.** It sits behind gates 1-5 by
registration, and it carries a dependency of its own: the elastic lifting must
be rebuilt with an explicit Kelvin/engineering/Voigt conversion and an asserted
interior residual. The dozen legacy `scripts/*_p43.py` retain 32 % of interior
residual and no number from them may be reused.

## Two harness defects, recorded

**The objective was numerically tiny and the optimiser stopped early.**
Displacements are in millimetres, so `J ~ 1e-13`, and L-BFGS-B's convergence
test `(f_k - f_k+1) / max(|f_k|, |f_k+1|, 1)` silently becomes *absolute*. The
first twin run terminated after 101 evaluations at **50 % field error**, looking
for all the world like a converged optimisation against an unidentifiable
representation. Scaling the objective by a fixed data norm — not by the current
iterate — took the same problem to 0.021 %. The plateau was an artefact, and had
the run been reported as it stood it would have produced a false negative on the
central question of the project.

**A premature reading of that run.** The early termination was diagnosed while
the process was still running and the diagnosis was briefly withdrawn on the
evidence that it had not stopped. It had not stopped *yet*. The correct
conclusion needed the exact Gauss-Newton arm, which does not depend on any
optimiser's stopping rule, and that is the arm that settled it.

## One registered detail corrected

The preregistration says the gradient is tested at `q = 1` and `q = 3`. The
basis is a tensor product, so degree one gives **four** modes per patch, not
three. `q = 4` was tested instead. This is a richer test than the one
registered, not a weaker one, and no threshold moved.

## What this authorises, and what it does not

Authorised: a rich local representation. The mechanical cost does not grow with
the coefficient count — 64 against 4096 gave identical Newton and Krylov counts
— and now the inverse is shown to be well posed at the same time. That is the
combination the programme needed.

Not authorised: any claim about 316L, about real DIC, or about `q > 1`. The twin
was generated by the exact forward model being fitted, which is the cleanest
possible setting and deliberately so — it isolates the inverse from model error.
Real data bring noise, and the spectrum says the directions between 1e-2 and
1e-3 of the leading singular value are the ones that will go first.

## Next, in order

2. Rebuild the elastic lifting with an asserted conversion, then gate 6.
3. Only then the generator: `a_jk = g_theta(z, S_n)`. The inverse chain it will

   to the generator rather than to the mechanics, the adjoint or the
   parameterisation.

The reduced integration domain stays closed. Its registered reopening condition
is in the preregistration and is unaffected by anything measured here.
