# SRIX-REGM exact-twin preregistration

Date: 2026-08-23  
Status: **frozen before the first SRIX twin result**

## Question

Does the reconditioned weak-equilibrium residual identify the sensitive
combinations of `(tau0, R, Q, b)` when displacement data are generated by the
same qualified SRIX law and full nonlinear mechanical solver?

## Twin 0

- structured `8 x 8` development grid, followed by timing-only `20 x 20` and
  `100 x 100` local replays;
- two independent TRI2 material states per pixel;
- four prescribed FCC orientations tiled spatially, fixed before results;
- non-proportional affine boundary path containing tension, transverse strain
  and shear, replayed in eight increments;
- truth: registered default SRIX preset;
- plane stress: qualified 3D condensed SRIX backend;
- observations: exact nodal displacement, identity transfer and identity
  whitener;
- primary reconditioner: orientation-dependent cubic plane-stress elasticity;
- sensitivity: homogeneous isotropic `K0`;
- no noise and no DIC transfer in this first gate.

The initial point is the truth multiplied respectively by

```text
tau0: 1.25, R: 0.80, Q: 1.30, b: 0.75.
```

`C`, `d`, cubic elasticity and the seven FCC interaction coefficients remain
fixed. Positive parameters are optimized in log coordinates. Bounds are a
factor four below and above the registered truth; they will not be changed
after seeing the result.

## Finite differences and SVD

Central differences are compared at log-coordinate steps
`1e-2`, `3e-3`, and `1e-3`. A column is considered on a plateau when the
relative change between the last two estimates is at most `2 %`, unless its
norm is below `1e-10` of the largest column and is explicitly declared
numerically null.

The numerical rank uses a relative singular-value threshold of `1e-6`. Only
the corresponding right-singular subspace may be judged as recovered. No
unique value is assigned along a null direction.

## Gate criteria

1. At the true parameters, pseudo-displacement RMS is at most `1e-8 mm` or is
   quantitatively explained by the forward-solver tolerance.
2. At least two theta4 sensitivity directions are non-null.
3. Moving along every retained singular direction increases the objective on
   at least one side of the truth.
4. Deterministic least squares from the frozen initial point reduces the
   residual norm by at least a factor ten.
5. The relative error projected onto the identifiable right-singular subspace
   is below `10 %`.

Failure is recorded as a negative result; no P43 identification follows.

## Later ranking gate, frozen now

On a small twin population generated independently of optimization, proceed to
P43 only if the REGM and full-FEMU rankings satisfy all of:

- Spearman correlation at least `0.80`;
- Pearson correlation at least `0.70` after logarithmic normalization of the
  positive objectives;
- at least three common points in their respective best-five sets.

The performance target is a median REGM evaluation at least five times faster
than a full forward trajectory. Missing this target triggers profiling, not a
scientific rejection by itself.

## Next gates

After Twin 0 only, repeat with the preregistered qualified transfer and then
with qualified noise. The exact-twin thresholds are not retroactively changed.
