# Preregistration — SRIX-REGM observation-placement ablation

Date: 2026-08-24

This experiment reuses the 20 off-truth M8 candidates already generated for the
exact and observed REGM/FEMU ranking gates. It does not launch a new mechanical
forward solve and it does not identify parameters.

## Question

The existing observed-space experiment applies the qualified affine-preserving
DIC transfer before the SRIX replay and again to the pseudo-displacement used
for scoring. This ablation separates:

1. the effect of changing the constitutive replay history;
2. the effect of applying an observation operator to the pseudo-displacement;
3. the difference between periodic FFT transfer and affine-preserving transfer.

The definitive comparison target is the already archived observed full-FEMU
ranking (`T1_transfer`), computed with the affine-preserving transfer and the
same 20 candidates.

## Frozen variants

| ID | SRIX replay history | Score transfer | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | raw exact twin `u*` | identity | archived exact-space reference |
| B-periodic | raw exact twin `u*` | periodic `apply` | scoring-transfer ablation |
| B-affine | raw exact twin `u*` | affine-preserving `apply_without_wrap` | scoring-transfer ablation |
| C | affine-preserving `O(u*)` | identity | constitutive-input bias |
| D-periodic | affine-preserving `O(u*)` | periodic `apply` | combined placement and scoring |
| D-affine | affine-preserving `O(u*)` | affine-preserving `apply_without_wrap` | existing observed REGM path |

For B and D, both transfer implementations are evaluated. No variant is
selected from its agreement with the target; all are reported.

## Acceptance and interpretation

For each variant report Spearman correlation, log-objective Pearson
correlation, and best-five overlap against the frozen observed FEMU ranking.
The existing ranking thresholds (`rho >= 0.80`, log-Pearson `>= 0.70`, overlap
`>= 3/5`) are descriptive only here; they do not authorize P43 identification.

At the truth, report the pseudo-displacement generated by C. Compare its
trajectory RMS with the candidate-to-candidate spread in C. A bias comparable
to that spread indicates that the observation operator changes the mechanical
signal at the scale being identified.

No new claim about SRIX parameters is allowed from this ablation alone.
